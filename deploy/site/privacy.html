遥应隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日 · English

遥应（Teleply，下称“我们”）由曾环宇运营。遥应让你在手机上查看和操作自己电脑上的 AI 编程助手。这份政策说明我们的服务器存了什么、转发什么、交给了谁，以及你怎样删除。

一、对话内容不留在我们的服务器上

你在手机上看到的对话，发出的文字、照片、文件和语音，都在你的手机和你自己的电脑之间传送。我们的中转服务器（阿里云，中国广州）只负责转发，不保存这些内容。传输全程用 HTTPS 加密，但不是端到端加密：内容在中转服务器上解密后再转发出去，我们不查看，也不保存。

二、服务器上保存的信息

  • 账号：账号编号、你关联的邮箱、密码的加密摘要（用 scrypt 计算，不保存密码本身）。
  • 登录状态：登录凭证的摘要，7 天后失效。
  • 你绑定的电脑：电脑名称和连接凭证的摘要。
  • 流量统计：每台电脑每天的请求次数和上传、下载的字节数，用来防止滥用和排查问题。
  • 通知：你在 iPhone 上开启遥应通知后，保存这台手机的推送标识（由苹果分配）、机型名称、App 版本和语言。为了在锁屏和灵动岛上显示进度，还会暂存进行中对话的标题和状态，对话停止更新一天后删除。
  • 防滥用记录：为限制过于频繁的请求，保存 IP 地址和账号经过摘要处理后的记录，最多 24 小时。
  • 邮箱验证：验证码的摘要，30 分钟后失效。

中转服务不记录访问日志。

三、会交给第三方的信息

  • 阿里云：我们的服务器和安装包存放在阿里云。
  • 苹果推送服务：开启通知后，通知内容（App 名称、电脑名称、对话标题和状态）经苹果送到你的 iPhone。
  • 邮件服务商：发送验证码邮件时，你的邮箱地址和验证码会经过发信邮箱的服务商。
  • 你电脑上的程序：你从手机发出的内容，最后交给你电脑上的 AI 编程助手和语音服务处理。这些程序由你自己安装和设置，各有各的隐私政策，我们不经手。
  • iPhone 语音识别：电脑没有开语音服务时，遥应用 iPhone 自带的语音识别把语音转成文字，由苹果按它的规则处理。

我们不出售你的信息，不做广告追踪，也不使用第三方统计工具。

四、手机上保存的信息

登录凭证保存在系统的加密存储里（iPhone 的钥匙串、安卓的加密存储），草稿和设置保存在手机上，这些不上传。

五、权限

  • 相机：拍照后作为附件发给电脑上的对话。
  • 麦克风和语音识别：语音输入。
  • 通知：对话进度提醒。

这些权限只在你用到相关功能时才请求，随时可以在系统设置里关闭。

六、删除账号和你的信息

在 App 里打开 设置 → 账号 → 删除账号，或在网页“我的电脑”右上角的菜单里选“删除账号”。输入密码确认后，服务器上的账号、邮箱、登录状态、绑定的电脑、流量统计和通知设置立即删除，不能恢复。电脑上的文件和对话不受影响。

你也可以写信给我们，查询、更正或删除你的信息。

七、未成年人

遥应面向成年人。不满 14 周岁的未成年人请不要注册。

八、变更与联系

这份政策有变化时，会在本页公布并更新上面的日期。有问题请写信到 390116927@qq.com。

Teleply Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Teleply (遥应, “we”) is run by Zeng Huanyu (曾环宇). Teleply lets you follow and answer the AI coding assistants running on your own computer from your phone. This policy says what our server keeps, what it passes along, who else gets anything, and how to delete it.

1. Your conversations are not kept on our server

The conversations you read and the text, photos, files and voice you send travel between your phone and your own computer. Our relay server (Alibaba Cloud, Guangzhou, China) only forwards them and does not store them. Everything travels over HTTPS, but this is not end-to-end encryption: content is decrypted on the relay before it is forwarded. We do not read or keep it.

2. What the server keeps

  • Account: an account ID, the email address you link, and a scrypt hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Sign-ins: hashes of sign-in tokens, which expire after 7 days.
  • Your computers: each computer’s name and a hash of its connection key.
  • Traffic counts: per computer and day, the number of requests and bytes sent and received, to prevent abuse and find problems.
  • Notifications: when you turn on Teleply notifications on an iPhone, its push token (issued by Apple), model name, app version and language. To show progress on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, the titles and states of running conversations are kept until a day after they stop changing.
  • Abuse limits: hashed records of IP addresses and accounts, kept for at most 24 hours, to limit requests that come too fast.
  • Email checks: hashes of verification codes, which expire after 30 minutes.

The relay service keeps no access logs.

3. What goes to others

  • Alibaba Cloud hosts our server and installer downloads.
  • Apple Push Notification service: with notifications on, the notice (app name, computer name, conversation title and state) reaches your iPhone through Apple.
  • Email provider: verification emails pass your address and the code through our mail provider.
  • Programs on your computer: what you send from the phone ends up with the AI coding assistants and speech service on your computer. You install and set them up yourself; their own privacy policies apply, and we are not involved.
  • iPhone speech recognition: when your computer has no speech service, Teleply uses the iPhone’s own speech recognition, which Apple handles under its rules.

We do not sell your information, track you for advertising, or use third-party analytics.

4. What stays on your phone

Sign-in tokens are kept in the system’s encrypted storage (the iPhone Keychain, Android’s encrypted storage); drafts and settings stay on the phone and are not uploaded.

5. Permissions

Camera: to send a photo to a conversation on your computer. Microphone and speech recognition: voice input. Notifications: progress alerts. Each is asked for only when you use that feature and can be turned off in the system settings at any time.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete Account; on the web, open the menu at the top right of “My Computers” and choose Delete Account. After you confirm with your password, your account, email, sign-ins, computers, traffic counts and notification settings are deleted from the server at once and cannot be restored. Files and conversations on your computer are not touched.

You can also write to us to see, correct or delete your information.

7. Children

Teleply is meant for adults. Children under 14 should not sign up.

8. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date. Questions: 390116927@qq.com.
