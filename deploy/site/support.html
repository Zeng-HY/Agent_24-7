遥应是做什么的

遥应让你在手机上查看和回复自己电脑上 AI 编程助手的对话。手机和电脑都要装遥应：电脑端负责连接，手机用来看和回复。

怎样开始

  1. 在 Windows 电脑上安装遥应电脑端（首页有下载链接）。
  2. 在手机上安装遥应，注册账号并登录。
  3. 在电脑端点“绑定”，用同一个账号登录并确认。之后手机的“我的电脑”里就会出现这台电脑。

电脑显示离线

看看电脑是否开着、遥应电脑端是否在运行、网络是否正常。电脑睡眠时连不上。

忘记密码

在登录页点“忘记密码？”，用邮箱收到的验证码设置新密码。

删除账号

App 里打开 设置 → 账号 → 删除账号；网页上在“我的电脑”右上角的菜单里选“删除账号”。删除后不能恢复，电脑上的文件和对话不受影响。详见隐私政策。

Help and contact

What Teleply does

Teleply lets you read and answer the conversations of the AI coding assistants on your own computer from your phone. Install Teleply on both: the computer app keeps the connection, the phone app is where you read and reply.

Getting started

  1. Install the Teleply computer app on a Windows PC (download link on the home page).
  2. Install Teleply on your phone, create an account and sign in.
  3. In the computer app, click “Bind”, sign in with the same account and confirm. The computer then shows up under “My Computers” on the phone.

The computer shows as offline

Check that the computer is on, the Teleply computer app is running and the network works. A sleeping computer cannot be reached.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in page, tap “Forgot password?” and set a new one with the code sent to your email.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. On the web: the menu at the top right of “My Computers” → Delete Account. This cannot be undone; files and conversations on your computer are not touched. See the privacy policy.
